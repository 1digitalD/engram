Annotations

  • 1Projects and areas collapse into one container: a Space, optionally with a finish line (outcome + target). Same surface either way — one less filing taxonomy to learn.
  • 2Standing orders: human-written, Space-scoped autonomy granted in advance. Every action they cause is attributed back to the order that authorized it.
  • 3The Brief is the highest-leverage AI artifact: the one-minute re-load after two weeks away. Clearly ✦-marked, timestamped, regenerable — never masquerading as human-written.
  • 4Citation chips (e41) link every claim to immutable Stream entries or Ledger events. No receipts, no claim (glass-box rule 6).
  • 5Delegation-to-agent is one click on any commitment you own; the row becomes a live run chip. Run outputs still arrive as proposals — delegation never buys silent writes.
  • 6The Spine is the “shared surface” made literal: captures, agent runs, proposals, and human decisions interleave in one attributed timeline. Violet dots = AI events.
  • 7Consequential changes (here: status) surface as inline proposals in the flow of work — the user is never forced to a separate queue to stay in control. The same proposal also sits in Review (Mockup 3).
  • 8The capture from Mockup 2 arrives here automatically via a standing order — annotate-tier linking, marked and undoable. Capture required zero filing; the Space still caught it.
  • 9The decision log makes the Space referenceable, not just searchable: what was decided, with whom, when, with a receipt to the exact words — and supersession links instead of silent replacement. “Why did we decide X?” is answered here in seconds.
  • 10Accountability lives in the Dossier too: every waiting-on shows its owner, its origin, and its age, with the receipt-based nudge one click away. The same items roll up per-person on the People surface and ripen onto Today.
