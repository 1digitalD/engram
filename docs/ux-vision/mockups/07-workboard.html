Annotations

  • 1One filter bar over the whole portfolio: every open commitment across every Space and person. Overdue / stale / blocked / at-risk are derived states with counts — “what's trailing” is one click, not a saved search.
  • 2Grouping pivots between by Space and by Person. “What is Priya carrying across everything?” and “what's open on Acme?” are the same screen, one toggle apart.
  • 3At-risk is derived, with a stated reason — finish-line proximity vs. aged commitments and a quiet Spine — never a status dropdown someone forgot to update. The flag links to its evidence.
  • 4Fast paths, inline on any row: Done ✓ and Log update each write a human-authored Ledger event (“closed by you, here, now”) — no form, no silent field edit. Same affordances on the Dossier and Today.
  • 5An explicit follow-up marker (“📌 nudge Fri”, “📌 discuss in next 1:1”) — human-set, one line. It fires into Today on its date and rides into meeting prep for its person. The complement to passive ripening.
  • 6Add-a-commitment inline, right where it belongs — a Ledger event with you as author. Capture is not the only door.
  • 7The monthly horizon of insights: portfolio health as a briefing, not a dashboard — quiet people, at-risk Spaces, idle themes, unowned work. Daily watch items live on Today; the weekly digest lives in Review. Same signals, three packagings.
  • 8Themes: forward-looking intent that isn't work yet — a name, a sentence, an optional horizon, plus any decisions and questions that have attached. Visible next to active work without pretending to be in motion.
  • 9When work starts, promote to Space — history comes along. Until then no Gantt bars, no swimlanes: enough structure for planning, deliberately short of a roadmap product.
